## 01. FORMULATION SCIENCE

### Better together. By design.

**An intelligent formula is more than a list of ingredients.**

Different nutrients have different roles, recommended intakes and formulation requirements. More is not always better, and not every combination is equally suitable.

Our science-led formulation framework considers:

- **Evidence-based dosing** — guided by established nutritional reference values and appropriate intake limits.
- **Ingredient compatibility** — evaluating how selected nutrients may interact within a formula.
- **Form and stability** — considering the characteristics of each nutrient and its suitability for the delivery format.
- **Balanced selection** — helping avoid unnecessary overlap with existing supplements.*

**The right ingredients. The right amounts. A formula with a reason.***

## 02. PRECISION DOSING

### One tear. One defined daily serving.

**Precision should be measured, not assumed.**

Every VITA TAPE daily serving is designed around a defined nutrient specification. Seven individual colour lanes come together in one connected ribbon, with each transverse tear line marking a complete daily portion.*

Our precision framework connects three essential elements:

**01 — Controlled Formulation**

**02 — Consistent Portioning**  
Each daily segment is designed to contain the specified amount of each selected nutrient.*

**03 — Analytical Verification**

## 03. INGREDIENT PURITY

### Purity is a standard. Not a slogan.

**What goes into your supplement matters as much as what it delivers.**

Our ingredient philosophy places quality, traceability and scientific evaluation before marketing trends.

The VITA TAPE ingredient quality framework is designed around four principles:*

**Verified Identity**  
Clearly defined ingredient identity and composition.

**High-Purity Sourcing**  
Raw materials selected against ingredient-specific purity and potency specifications.

**Contaminant Control**  
Appropriate screening for potential contaminants, including relevant heavy metals and microbiological risks.

**Traceable Quality**  
Supplier documentation, batch traceability and laboratory quality records.

We believe a cleaner formula is not simply one with fewer ingredients. It is one where every ingredient has a purpose and meets a defined quality standard.

**Nothing unnecessary. Nothing left to guesswork.***

## 04. EDIBLE MATERIAL SCIENCE

### Advanced nutrition needs advanced materials.

**Not a tablet. Not an ordinary gummy.**

VITA TAPE reimagines supplement delivery through an engineered edible matrix: a soft, flexible structure designed to carry selected nutrients in one connected, chewable ribbon.*

The material system is developed around multiple performance requirements:

**Flexibility** — designed to roll without losing its intended form.

**Cohesion** — designed to keep the connected ribbon intact during normal handling.

**Portionability** — designed for a clean, complete daily serving.

**Texture** — a soft, enjoyable chew.

**Formulation compatibility** — considering the interaction between the edible matrix and incorporated nutrients.

Scientific advances in food-grade gels, food rheology and multi-material fabrication provide the foundations for exploring this new delivery format.

**Material science you can see. Nutrition you can enjoy.***

## 06. FRESHNESS ENGINEERING

### Freshness deserves its own technology.

**Nutritional quality doesn't end when manufacturing is complete.**

Vitamins and other nutritional ingredients can respond differently to oxygen, moisture, light and temperature. Protecting a formula therefore requires a coordinated approach to material selection, storage and packaging.

VITA TAPE introduces a weekly refill concept designed around seven days of use.*

**Seven Daily Portions**  
One weekly refill contains seven intended daily servings.

**Individually Sealed Refills**  
Four separate weekly refills form a 28-day supply, allowing unopened refills to remain sealed until needed.*

**Protective Packaging Design**  
Packaging is designed with oxygen, moisture and light exposure in mind.*

**Stability-Led Development**  
Nutrient retention, product texture and real-world opening conditions are considered within the product validation framework.*

**A fresh start, every seven days.***
