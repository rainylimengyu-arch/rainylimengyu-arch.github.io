# Questionnaire research and implementation decisions

Research date: 2026-10-20. Scope: live questionnaire interaction, official brand descriptions, original BUBU code and official/primary nutrition evidence. This is a comparison of observable product flows, not a reverse engineering of a proprietary recommendation algorithm or a clinical validation of commercial claims.

| Source | What was verified | Decision for VITA TAPE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Nourished quiz](https://get-nourished.com/pages/quiz) | Walked the live five-stage form using a fictional demo profile. Stages cover personal context, everyday life, diet, health and stack preferences. Its final stage asks for priorities, flavour and email. No email was supplied and no order or newsletter subscription was created; the private scoring / personalised result algorithm was not inspected. | Use clear groups, manageable question screens and goal prioritisation. Keep BUBU's more detailed portions, alternatives and safeguards; do not copy commercial claims or require an email. |
| [Persona assessment](https://www.personanutrition.com/start-the-assessment/) and [questionnaire help](https://support.personanutrition.com/hc/en-us/sections/115001231327-Questionnaire) | Official descriptions combine diet, lifestyle, goals, allergies and medicines, then explain/customise recommendations with professional support. Brand claims about interaction-database size are not independently audited. | Ask medicines, existing supplements and exclusions, show reasons, allow discussion choices; do not claim an equivalent clinical drug-interaction database. |
| [Vitl personalised pack](https://vitl.com/products/personalised-pack), [official consultation overview](https://vitl.com/blogs/vitl-life/vitl-complete-guide-to-personalised-vitamins-and-supplements) | Official descriptions combine dietary/lifestyle questions with supplement suggestions and dietary advice; tests are a separate optional input. | Pair the supplement shortlist with the full food-source assessment. Keep Plus reports optional and do not imply uploaded files were analysed. |
| BUBU mini-program `pages/questionnaire/data/questions-v6.js`, `engine/flow-v6.js`, `engine/recommendations-v5.js` and `engine/recommendations-v6.js` | Read the current local implementation and reused its dependency snapshot. It already has source coverage, conditional goal follow-ups, existing-product checks and functional/botanical branches. | Reuse the actual rule engine and retain its reasons and references. Add complete visible coverage and an auditable live-catalog decision layer rather than restricting recommendations to three nutrients. |

The agent-reach Jina backend was attempted but local DNS could not resolve it. Official pages were read through the available web reader and the supplied Nourished URL was inspected in the in-app browser. Research did not use private accounts, real user health data, customer testimonials or non-public competitor internals.

## Evidence checks behind the expansion

- [NIH ODS vitamin/mineral fact sheets](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/list-VitaminsMinerals/) and [multivitamin/mineral consumer sheet](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/MVMS-Consumer/): source context and product overlap are kept separate from a personal prescription. Broad restriction can support considering one basic product rather than automatically stacking many singles.
- [NIH ODS exercise and athletic performance](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ExerciseAndAthleticPerformance-HealthProfessional/): supports distinguishing stable strength/high-intensity training, meal convenience and prolonged sweating; no personal research dose is copied into the report.
- [2025 magnesium bisglycinate randomised trial, PMID 40918053](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12412596/): the 155-adult, four-week trial reported a modest self-reported sleep improvement. It does not establish a universal insomnia treatment or final Tape-form efficacy.
- [2019 L-theanine randomised crossover trial, PMID 31623400](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6836118/) and [GAD trial PMID 30580081](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30580081/): small non-clinical evidence is not an anxiety-disorder treatment claim.
- [NCCIH Asian ginseng](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/asian-ginseng), [NCCIH turmeric](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/turmeric) and [turmeric preparation exercise trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12498372/): botanical interest, actual context, medicines and product-specific safety matter; concentrated extracts differ from cooking ingredients.
- [2025 collagen meta-analysis, PMID 40324552](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40324552/): higher-quality and non-industry-funded subgroups did not show clear benefit. Collagen remains an optional, low-priority discussion item with uncertainty, not an anti-ageing promise.
- [NIH molybdenum](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Molybdenum-Consumer/), [chromium](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Chromium-Consumer/), [fluoride](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Fluoride-Consumer/) and [NHS Eatwell](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/food-guidelines-and-food-labels/the-eatwell-guide/): the wider coverage table includes directions for which routine supplementation is not established by this questionnaire. Fluoride requires water/dental context, and macros/water are not replaced by micronutrient gummies.

## What the result must communicate

The full assessment covers source decisions, relevant next steps, matching reasons, evidence, food alternatives, existing products and limits. It does not mean every row needs a supplement, that an unknown answer means deficiency, or that seven slots must be filled. A matching ingredient is distinguished from an available, confirmed final Tape form. English and Chinese share the same answers and eligibility decisions; raw user notes/file names are preserved.
